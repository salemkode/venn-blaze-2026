Venn is a Bitcoin Cash wallet for shared money. Each group agrees on who can spend and how much before the fund is created.

Consider a household fund. Each person can buy groceries within a budget. Larger expenses require a vote. The app shows the permissions and their trade-offs before anyone joins.

The creator chooses Shared, names the members, and sends an invitation. Each member reviews the rules and signs for their own seat. Creation waits for every acceptance.

The Bitcoin Cash covenant holds the fund. CashTokens carry the rules and the member state. The Android screenshot shows a separate check of native cryptography and secure storage.

Here is the spending rule in action with local test fixtures. The Bitcoin Cash virtual machine accepts a signed payment within budget. It rejects a transaction above the budget.

The shared-money prototype is available on Chipnet. A complete live two-wallet run and browser pairing are the next checks. Group approvals and recovery are covered at the contract level.

The Venn repository includes the source, a three-minute demo guide, and the commands to reproduce the contract checks.
